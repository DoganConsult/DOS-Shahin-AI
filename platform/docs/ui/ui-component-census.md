- `CORE_SHARED_UI` rows already live in `@dos/ui-system` or are package-shared; they are the canonical primitives.
- `LEGACY_DUPLICATE` rows must be replaced with the suggested `Dos*` target during page-by-page migration; do not delete until the page consuming them has been migrated and verified.

- `PRODUCT_THEME_UI` rows belong to product/brand surfaces (landing, marketing) and stay product-owned.
- `ORPHAN_DEAD` rows have no inbound import or route mount detected; confirm manually before archiving.
- `THIRD_PARTY_WRAPPER` rows wrap PrimeNG / ECharts / D3 and stay where they are; ensure they consume tokens.
- `UNKNOWN_REVIEW` rows require human classification.
